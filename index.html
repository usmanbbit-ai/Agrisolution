FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does one bale weigh?

Our RANG bales weigh 80-90 KG each. They are compacted at high density and wrapped for easy handling and storage.

How long does the silage last?

Our baled corn silage has a shelf life of 12+ months. The UV-protected wrapping keeps it fresh until you open it.

What is the nutritional value?

8-9% protein and 35% dry matter, with high digestibility and balanced energy and minerals. Wastage is only 2-3%.

Do you sell silage in bags?

Yes. Along with RANG 80-90 KG bales, our corn silage is now available in bags of approx. 50 KG (custom sizes on request): moisture 65-70%, protein 7-8.5%, dry matter 30-35%, pH 3.8-4.0.

Which animals is it for?

Cows, buffaloes, goats and sheep. It is a high-nutrition feed for better health and milk production.

Do you deliver?

Yes. We deliver all over Pakistan. Bulk orders are welcome, and field pickup is also available.

When can I order?

Any time. We are available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, all year round.
